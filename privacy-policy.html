Sampan Stories privacy policy

About this policy

Sampan Stories is developed by Moonlit Pond Studio. Contact: gray.standbyme@gmail.com. This policy is effective from 23 November 2026. It covers the browser preview and the Android app.

Game progress on your device

The game saves progress and preferences in the browser’s localStorage under tidepocket.v4 or in the Android app’s private WebView local storage. This includes catches, coins, upgrades, daily tasks, tutorial progress, sound choices and which pets you own. Android can back up the app’s local storage or transfer it to another Android device, depending on your device settings. Moonlit Pond Studio does not receive this save. The game has no accounts, studio-operated cloud saves, chat or player messaging.

Network requests

The Android app includes its fonts. The browser preview loads the fonts from the same host as the game. A hosted browser preview sends ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, to its hosting provider. Hosting logs and retention depend on the host.

Ads in the Android app

The browser preview uses a placeholder for optional rewarded ads. The Android app uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded video ads: you choose to watch one to get a reward, and the reward is given only when the ad reports it was completed. AdMob and Google’s advertising services may collect or share your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), your device’s advertising ID, device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostic information. They use it to show and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, personalise ads. The app does not itself send this information to Gray. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Consent and your choices

Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent about ads when you first open it, using Google’s User Messaging Platform. Where Google requires a way to change your choice later, a “Privacy choices” button appears in the settings menu (the gear) and opens that form again. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings. You can decline ads: they are always optional, and the game works without watching any.

Purchases in the Android app

Some pets are bought with a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google handles the payment and the receipt; Moonlit Pond Studio does not receive or store your card or payment details. The app stores only which pets you own, on your device. “Restore purchases” in the settings menu asks Google Play which pets you own and gives them back, for example after you reinstall the game or change phone. Refunds and payment questions are handled by Google Play.

Retention and deletion

Progress stays on your device until you clear the browser’s stored data for this site, or clear or uninstall the Android app, which deletes the local save. This cannot be undone through Moonlit Pond Studio. A copy of the Android save may also remain in your device backup. Manage it in your Android backup settings. Purchases are tied to your Google Play account, not to the save, so you can restore them. If you email support, Moonlit Pond Studio receives your email address and message and uses them to respond. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the request and applicable obligations; contact Gray to request deletion.

Security

Progress is stored on your device, so protect access to your device and browser. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure. External services are contacted over secure connections.

Audience

Sampan Stories is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and the app is not designed to collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 uses the game, contact Moonlit Pond Studio.

Changes and contact

Moonlit Pond Studio will update this policy when the game’s data practices change and date the revised policy. Questions or deletion requests: gray.standbyme@gmail.com.
